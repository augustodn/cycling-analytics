# Weekly Lunch Plan - Build to XCM 35km (Sep 20, 2026)

This 7-day lunch plan is timed for a 14:30 training start (aim to have lunch ~12:00-12:30 and a small pre-ride snack ~13:45). It follows the weekly templates in your build plan (hard sessions on Tuesday, long Saturday, gym on Monday/Thursday, Zone 2 midweek) and avoids high-heme iron foods because of hemochromatosis.

This is general nutrition guidance and not medical advice. Continue to follow your physician's or dietitian's recommendations for hemochromatosis and coordinate any major changes with them.

---

Key hemochromatosis rules
- Avoid high-heme foods: beef/asado, lamb, liver/organ meats, clams/oysters/mussels, canned sardines/anchovies. Avoid iron supplements and iron-fortified cereals unless cleared by your doctor.
- Prefer: poultry (pollo), turkey (pavo), white fish (merluza, trucha), eggs, dairy (queso fresco, yogurt), and plant proteins in moderation (legumes). Legumes contain non-heme iron; avoid pairing them with high vitamin-C foods at the same meal if you want to limit iron absorption.
- Tea/coffee with the meal reduce iron absorption (useful for hemochromatosis); vitamin C (orange, kiwi, fresh citrus juices) increases iron absorption - avoid large vitamin-C doses at lunch if the meal contains significant non-heme iron.
- Avoid frequent cooking in cast-iron cookware (it raises iron content in food).

Fueling targets (adjust to appetite & session length)
- Hard session (FTP/punchy): lunch carbs 70-110 g; protein 25-35 g; fat low-moderate.
- Long ride (2-3.5 h): lunch carbs 80-120 g; during-ride carbs 60-90 g/h (practice this in training).
- Zone-2 / moderate (75-120 min): lunch carbs 50-75 g.
- Gym or short session: lunch carbs 50-80 g; protein 25-35 g.
- Rest day: lighter - carbs 30-50 g, protein 25-35 g.

Timing rules
- Lunch: aim 12:00-12:30 for a 14:30 start. For heavy long rides, move lunch earlier (11:30-12:00) if needed.
- Pre-ride snack: simple carbs 20-40 g (banana, rice cake + honey, small bar) 30-45 min before.
- Post-ride: aim for ~20-30 g protein + 40-60 g carbs within 60 min.

---

Daily plan (eat lunch ~12:00-12:30; snack ~13:45; post-ride within 30-60 min)

## Monday
Meal: Grilled chicken breast (pechuga, 150-200 g) + 1.5 cups cooked white rice + roasted zucchini.
Why: Strength day (Gym A) needs protein for recovery and moderate carbs to fuel lifting and any easy spin.
Portions / estimate: ~70-80 g carbs, 30+ g protein.
Pre-ride snack (13:45): 1 banana or 1 rice cake with honey (20-30 g carbs).
Post-ride recovery: 200 g Greek yogurt or 250 ml low-fat milk + 1 slice white bread (20-30 g carbs + 20-25 g protein).

## Tuesday (Key interval / FTP builder)
Meal: White pasta (120-140 g dry / ~2 cups cooked) with grilled chicken (150 g) and roasted mushrooms. Light olive oil dressing.
Why: High-GI carbs, low fiber and moderate protein are ideal before an intense trainer session.
Portions / estimate: ~80-100 g carbs, 30 g protein.
Pre-ride snack (13:30-13:45): rice cake + honey or small banana (20-30 g carbs). If you use gels, pick one without added vitamin C.
Post-ride recovery: 300 ml chocolate milk or smoothie with banana + whey (~25-30 g protein + 40-50 g carbs).

## Wednesday (Long Zone 2)
Meal: Grilled merluza (200 g) + 2 medium boiled potatoes or 1.5 cups white rice + steamed green beans or carrots.
Why: Sustained aerobic ride needs steady carbs and lean protein; choose white potato/rice for easier digestion.
Portions / estimate: ~60-80 g carbs, 30 g protein.
Pre-ride snack (if ride >90 min): 45-60 min before: 1 banana + 2 rice cakes (30-45 g carbs).
Post-ride recovery: 150-200 g Greek yogurt + 40 g oats or a sandwich with jam and low-fat cheese.

## Thursday
Meal: Turkey (pavo) wrap: 120 g turkey breast in a white tortilla + 1 medium baked sweet potato (or 1 cup cooked rice) on the side.
Why: Gym B + short endurance - good balance of carbs and protein. Avoid spinach-heavy wraps if you are minimizing iron intake.
Portions / estimate: ~60-80 g carbs, 25-30 g protein.
Pre-ride snack: small banana or 1 energy bar (check ingredients for vitamin C).
Post-ride recovery: 2 boiled eggs + 1 slice white bread or 200 g cottage cheese + fruit (apple).

## Friday (Rest)
Meal: Omelette (2 eggs + 2 egg whites) with mushrooms, onions and queso fresco + 1 slice white bread or small roll.
Why: Lower carb day, maintain protein for recovery.
Portions / estimate: ~30-45 g carbs, 25-30 g protein.
Pre-ride snack: none needed unless you do a late session.
Post-lunch: light snack later if hungry (yogurt + a few nuts).

## Saturday (Long endurance / Race simulation)
Meal: Big pre-ride lunch - 2 cups cooked white rice + roasted chicken thigh (180 g) + pumpkin/roasted potato slices.
Why: Big carb load before a 2-3.5 h ride; this is the main fuel meal. Eat earlier (~11:45-12:15) if you feel heavy after eating.
Portions / estimate: ~100-120 g carbs, 30-35 g protein.
On-ride fueling: practice 60-90 g carbs/hour (mix of sports drink, bananas, energy chews/gels, rice cakes with honey). Avoid pairing those foods with a vitamin C drink if you want to limit iron absorption - choose sports drinks without added vitamin C if concerned.
Post-ride recovery: 300 ml chocolate milk + sandwich (30-50 g carbs + 20-30 g protein) or yogurt + oats + banana.

## Sunday (Rest / easy)
Meal: Cold chicken salad: leftover roasted chicken (120-150 g) + mixed lettuce, avocado, boiled egg + 1 slice white bread.
Why: Light, protein-forward, lower carbs to help recovery.
Portions / estimate: ~30-50 g carbs, 25-30 g protein.
Post-lunch: tea/coffee (helps inhibit iron absorption).

Quick recipes / swaps (Argentine staples)
- Empanadas: choose pollo or queso/verdura empanadas (avoid carne/viscera).
- Sandwiches: pan frances/medialuna or baguette with pollo/pavo and queso fresco.
- Fish: merluza or trucha are good; limit shellfish and sardines.
- Potatoes/pastas/rice: prefer white rice/pasta/potato pre-ride for easier digestion.
- Legumes: can be used in moderation but they raise non-heme iron - if you include lentejas/garbanzos, avoid citrus or vitamin-C juice with that same meal and prefer tea with the meal.

Shopping basics (week)
- Chicken breasts/thighs, turkey breast, merluza/trucha, eggs, Greek yogurt, white rice, dried pasta, potatoes & sweet potatoes, white tortillas / pan frances, bananas, rice cakes, oats, olive oil, lettuce/cucumber/mushrooms/zucchini, queso fresco.

Practical tips
- Lunch timing: target 12:00-12:30 for a 14:30 start; if heavy long ride, move lunch earlier to ~11:30-12:00.
- Pre-ride snack: simple carbs 20-40 g (banana, rice cake + honey, small bar) 30-45 min before.
- Post-ride: aim 20-30 g protein plus 40-60 g carbs within 60 min.
- Avoid citrus juices or high-vitamin-C meals at lunchtime if you want to reduce iron absorption. Tea/coffee with lunch is useful to blunt iron uptake (but test caffeine tolerance before hard sessions).
- Check product labels (some sports gels/drinks add vitamin C) - choose formulas without it if avoiding iron uptake.
- If you regularly undergo phlebotomy, coordinate larger carbohydrate intakes around those weeks with your clinician.
