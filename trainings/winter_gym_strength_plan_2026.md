# Revised Gym Plan - May to November 2026

## Purpose
- Support `trainings/season_plan_to_xcm_nov2026.md`.
- Build strength in winter without compromising the key bike sessions.
- Fit your Monday and Friday gym availability.
- Protect the ankle with controlled loading and minimal impact.

---

## Main Principles
- Keep `2` gym days most weeks: **Monday** and **Friday**.
- Make Monday the main lower-body day because Tuesday is only Zone 2.
- Use Friday as a low-fatigue upper-body / trunk / ankle day.
- Keep both sessions submaximal and avoid chasing failure.
- Stay `1-3 reps` away from failure.

---

## Seasonal Structure

### May To July - Strength Build
- **Frequency:** `2` sessions/week
- **Goal:** improve force production while preserving Tuesday bike quality
- **Best use:** one moderate lower-body exposure early in the week, one low-fatigue support session on Friday

### July To August - Strength Maintenance With FTP Focus
- **Frequency:** `2` sessions/week
- **Goal:** maintain or very slowly build strength without carrying fatigue into the bike work

### September To October - Race Support
- **Frequency:** `1.5-2` sessions/week
- **Goal:** keep the strength touch while shorter races become more frequent
- **Race week rule:** if racing on the weekend, Friday becomes mobility, trunk, and ankle only

### November - Peak Support
- **Frequency:** `1-2` short sessions/week
- **Goal:** arrive coordinated and strong, not tired
- **Final 10-12 days before the main race:** no heavy lifting

---

## Weekly Placement
- **Monday:** lower-body priority + easy Z2 ride
- **Friday:** upper-body priority + recovery bias

### Monday Rule
- Monday is the main lower-body stimulus of the week.
- Keep the ride easy enough that the lift still feels clean.

### Friday Rule
- Friday should never cost you quality on Saturday.
- Keep the legs light; upper body, trunk, ankle, and carries are the priority.

---

## Warm-Up
Use `8-10 min` before each session:
- `3-5 min` easy bike or brisk walk
- ankle rocks
- adductor rock-backs
- glute bridge `2 x 8`
- dead bug `2 x 6/side`
- `2-3` ramp-up sets on the first lift

---

## Monday - Lower-Body Priority
Use this as the default Monday session.

### Main lift - pick `1`
- safety-bar squat
- trap-bar deadlift
- leg press if ankle tolerance is limited

### Secondary lift - pick `1`
- Romanian deadlift
- hip thrust

### Single-leg lift - pick `1`
- split squat
- step-up
- split squat isometric hold if the ankle is sensitive

### Upper body - pick `1` pull and `1` push
- row or pulldown
- dumbbell bench, push-up, or landmine press

### Core and ankle
- `1` anti-rotation or anti-extension drill
- soleus/calf work

### Default prescription
- main lift: `3-4 x 4-5`
- secondary lift: `2-3 x 5-6`
- single-leg lift: `2 x 5-6/side`
- upper body: `2-3 x 6-10`
- core/calf: `2-3` sets

This session should feel productive, not crushing.

---

## Friday - Low-Fatigue Maintenance
Keep this session light enough that Saturday still works.

### Upper body - pick `2-3`
- chest-supported row
- one-arm row
- landmine press
- dumbbell shoulder press
- push-up
- face pull

### Trunk and ankle
- side plank or Pallof press
- suitcase carry or farmer carry
- calf/soleus
- tibialis or balance drill if useful

### Default prescription
- upper body: `2-3 x 6-10`
- trunk/ankle: `2-3` sets

If Saturday is a race or big simulation, keep Friday to upper body, trunk, and ankle only.

---

## Progression Rules
- Start each new phase conservative.
- Add load only when Thursday quality and Saturday endurance stay stable.
- Progress by small load jumps, one extra rep, or one extra set.
- Every `4th` week, reduce gym volume by about `30%`.

---

## Ankle-Safe Swaps
- squat -> leg press or box squat
- lunge -> shortened split squat or isometric hold
- step-up -> lower box step-up
- dynamic calf raises -> isometric calf holds
- jump work -> remove

If pain goes above `3/10`, reduce range, reduce load, or remove the lower-body work for that day.

---

## Example Week

### Monday Before Over-Unders
- safety-bar squat `3 x 5`
- Romanian deadlift `2 x 6`
- split squat `2 x 5/side`
- chest-supported row `3 x 8`
- dumbbell bench `3 x 8`
- Pallof press `2 x 10/side`
- seated soleus raise `2-3 x 12`

### Monday Before 40/20s
- hip thrust `2 x 5`
- one-arm row `3 x 8/side`
- landmine press `3 x 8/side`
- face pull `2 x 12`
- side plank `2 x 30 s/side`
- calf isometric `2 x 30 s`

### Friday
- chest-supported row `3 x 8`
- push-up `3 x 8-10`
- face pull `2 x 12`
- suitcase carry `2 x 20-30 m/side`
- soleus raise `2 x 12`

---

## Final Guidance
- The gym is helping if Thursday quality stays good and Saturday endurance still feels productive.
- Monday is the lower-body day; Friday is the support day.
- Friday should support Saturday, not compete with it.
- When in doubt, do less with the legs.
