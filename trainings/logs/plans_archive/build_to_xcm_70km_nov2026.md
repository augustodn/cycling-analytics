# Build Plan for XCM 70km Races - September 21 to Late November, 2026

## Race Details
- **Primary event:** XCM `70 km` on MTB
- **Primary race window:** mid-November 2026
- **Working target date:** Sunday, November 15, 2026
- **Optional second event:** late November 2026
- **Working secondary date:** Sunday, November 29, 2026
- **Expected duration:** roughly `3-4 h`, depending on course and conditions
- **Context:** follows the September 20 `35 km` A race and shifts the focus toward marathon durability, fueling, and repeatable MTB pacing over longer duration

---

## Block Priorities
1. Recover from the September race without losing aerobic momentum.
2. Extend long-ride durability from `~90 min` race readiness toward `3-4 h` XCM readiness.
3. Raise sustainable sub-threshold and threshold power for long climbs and repeated fatigue-resistant efforts.
4. Keep one punchy touch so short climbs and technical exits do not become a weakness.
5. Rehearse fueling for long MTB racing: start early, target `60-90 g carbs/h`, and use only well-tolerated products.
6. Protect the ankle by preferring seated power, smooth cadence, and fatigue control over brute torque.

---

## Planning Principles
- **Bias:** endurance durability first, FTP support second, punchy work third.
- **Weekly pattern:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday riding; Friday and Sunday mostly rest.
- **Intensity distribution target:** about `80%` easy aerobic, `15%` threshold/sub-threshold, `5%` punchy/high intensity.
- **Bike use:**
  - **Road bike:** clean aerobic support and long steady work.
  - **Smart trainer:** controlled threshold/FTP support when needed.
  - **MTB:** long specificity rides, technical pacing, terrain tolerance, and fueling practice.
- **Ankle rules:** stay seated on hard efforts whenever possible, avoid grinding low cadence under heavy fatigue, and reduce intensity if pain exceeds `3/10` or next-morning stiffness increases.

## Macro Structure

### Phase 1 - Post-September Reset
- **Dates:** September 21-27
- **Goal:** recover from the September 20 race and reset before the marathon-oriented build.
- **Weekly volume:** `4-5 h`
- **Main work:** easy Zone 2, mobility, and optional light gym reintroduction.

### Phase 2 - Durability Build I
- **Dates:** September 28-October 18
- **Goal:** rebuild volume and extend long-ride comfort.
- **Weekly volume:** `7.5-9 h`
- **Priorities:**
  - `1` long ride every week, progressing from `2.5 h` toward `3.5 h`
  - `1` threshold-support session each week
  - `1` steady aerobic support ride
  - `1` MTB skills/endurance ride when possible
- **Quality session examples:**
  - `3 x 15 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - `2 x 20 min @ 90-92% FTP`
  - `3 x 12 min upper Zone 2 / low tempo on climbs`

### Phase 3 - Durability Build II
- **Dates:** October 19-November 1
- **Goal:** make long rides more race-like and improve fatigue resistance.
- **Weekly volume:** `8.5-10 h`
- **Priorities:**
  - long ride grows toward `3.5-4 h`
  - add race-specific steady blocks after `90+ min` of riding
  - keep one short punchy session every `7-10` days
- **Quality session examples:**
  - `2 x 25 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - `3 x 15 min @ 90-94% FTP`
  - `6 x 1 min @ 118-122% FTP / 2.5 min easy` only as a maintenance touch

### Phase 4 - Specific Marathon Build
- **Dates:** November 2-8
- **Goal:** most race-specific week of the block.
- **Weekly volume:** `8-9 h`
- **Priorities:**
  - one long MTB ride with a `60-90 min` race-feel block inside it
  - one threshold session
  - one punchy maintenance session
  - full fueling rehearsal

### Phase 5 - Taper to Primary 70km Race
- **Dates:** November 9-15
- **Goal:** reduce fatigue while keeping the system awake for the mid-November event.

### Phase 6 - Recover and Rebuild for Optional Second 70km Race
- **Dates:** November 16-29
- **Goal:** recover from race 1, then sharpen again if the second race becomes real.
- **Weekly volume:**
  - recovery week start: `3.5-5 h`

## Fueling Priorities
- Practice `60-90 g carbs/h` every Saturday long ride from early October onward.
- Start intake in the first `15-20 min`, not after fatigue begins.
- Rehearse bottle, gel, and carrying strategy on the MTB.
- Do not test new products in the final `10-14` days before either race.

---

## Monitoring Rules
- Keep pain at or below `3/10` during and after rides.
- Watch next-morning ankle stiffness after long rides and punchy Thursdays.
- If fatigue rises, cut the Thursday intensity before reducing the long ride.
- If long-ride recovery worsens, shorten the race-feel block rather than skipping fueling practice.
- Every `3-4` weeks, reduce total volume by about `25-30%` if signs of accumulated fatigue appear.

## Summary
- **Main objective:** convert your shorter-race fitness into `70 km` XCM durability.
