# Build Plan for XCM Tune-Up Race - August 17 to September 6, 2026

## Race Details
- **Event:** MTB/XCM tune-up race
- **Race Date:** Sunday, September 6, 2026
- **Status:** Tentative first target
- **Race character:** Similar to the May 10 race: repeated punchy efforts, frequent re-accelerations, technical pacing, and the need to recover quickly between surges
- **Role in the season:** `B` race inside the larger build toward the `A` race on September 20

---

## Block Priorities
1. Convert winter FTP/base work into race-specific MTB power.
2. Improve repeatability of `40-60 s` and `1-2 min` efforts without overloading the ankle.
3. Rehearse MTB pacing, corner exits, and fueling.
4. Arrive sharp enough to race well, but not so deep that recovery for September 20 is compromised.

---

## Session Rules
- Prefer seated accelerations.
- Use the MTB whenever practical on Thursdays and Saturdays.
- Limit all-out standing efforts.
- If ankle discomfort rises, reduce rep count before increasing recovery days.

---

## Week 1 - Specific Build
- **Dates:** August 17-23
- **Target volume:** `7.5-8.5 h`
- **Goal:** reintroduce punchy specificity after the base-heavy winter.

### Monday
- **Activity:** Rest or `40 min` easy spin

### Tuesday
- **Bike:** Smart trainer
- **Duration:** `60-70 min`
- **Session:** Seated repeatability
  - `15 min` warm-up
  - `2 sets of 8 x (40 s hard / 20 s easy)`
  - Hard reps at `120-125% FTP`
  - `5-6 min` easy between sets
  - `10 min` cool-down

### Wednesday
- **Bike:** Road
- **Duration:** `90-120 min`
- **Session:** Zone 2 high cadence

### Thursday
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Duration:** `75-90 min`
- **Session:** Short climbs and exits
  - `15 min` warm-up
  - `8 x 1 min hard / 2-3 min easy`
  - Finish with `20-25 min` technical riding at controlled pace

### Friday
- **Activity:** Rest

### Saturday
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Duration:** `2.5-3 h`
- **Session:** Endurance plus race block
  - Mostly Zone 2
  - `1 x 30-40 min` at lap feel
  - Practice `60-90 g carbs/h`

### Sunday
- **Activity:** Rest

## Week 2 - Specific Build
- **Dates:** August 24-30
- **Target volume:** `7-8 h`
- **Goal:** hardest race-specific week, but still controlled.

### Monday
- **Activity:** Rest or `30-45 min` recovery spin

### Tuesday
- **Bike:** Smart trainer or road
- **Duration:** `60-70 min`
- **Session:** One-minute repeatability
  - `15 min` warm-up
  - `8-10 x (1 min @ 118-122% FTP / 2 min easy)`
  - seated throughout
  - `10 min` cool-down

### Wednesday
- **Bike:** Road
- **Duration:** `75-105 min`
- **Session:** Zone 2 aerobic support

### Thursday
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Duration:** `75-90 min`
- **Session:** Threshold support plus skills
  - `15 min` warm-up
  - `3 x 8-10 min @ 95-100% FTP` on terrain that allows control
  - `4-5 min` easy between reps
  - finish with skills / exits / traction work

### Friday
- **Activity:** Rest

### Saturday
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Duration:** `2-2.5 h`
- **Session:** Recon or simulation ride
  - mostly Zone 2
  - one simulation lap or `25-35 min` race block
  - rehearse race-day fueling and pacing

### Sunday
- **Activity:** Rest

## Week 3 - Taper Week
- **Dates:** August 31-September 6
- **Target volume:** `4-5 h`
- **Goal:** keep the system awake while dropping fatigue.

### Monday
- **Activity:** Rest or `30 min` easy spin

### Tuesday
- **Bike:** Smart trainer
- **Duration:** `50-60 min`
- **Session:** Short sharpening work
  - `15 min` warm-up
  - `4 x 2 min @ 110-115% FTP`
  - `4 min` easy between reps
  - easy cool-down

### Wednesday
- **Bike:** Road
- **Duration:** `60-75 min`
- **Session:** Easy to steady Zone 2

### Thursday
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Duration:** `50-60 min`
- **Session:** Race-feel activation
  - `15 min` easy
  - `3 x 3 min` at race feel
  - `4 min` easy between reps
  - `3-4 x 10 s` sharp but controlled accelerations

### Friday
- **Activity:** Rest

### Saturday
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Duration:** `30-40 min`
- **Session:** Openers
  - easy riding
  - `3 x 1 min` at race pace
  - `3 x 10 s` sharp accelerations
  - finish wanting more

### Sunday - RACE DAY
- **Bike:** MTB
- **Execution notes:**
  - first third: controlled and smooth
  - middle third: press key climbs and exits
  - final third: spend matches only where they matter
  - fuel early if race duration goes beyond `75-80 min`

---

## Recovery Into September 20
- **September 7:** full rest
- **September 8:** `45-60 min` very easy
- **September 9:** easy Zone 2 only if legs are normal
- Then resume the September 20 build with one threshold touch, one punchy activation, and a clean taper.

---

## Summary
- This race is a `B` race: race it well, but do not bury yourself.
- The main point is to test punchiness, pacing, and technical execution.
- Recover quickly afterward so the September 20 target remains the priority.
