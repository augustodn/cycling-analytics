# Build Plan for XCM 35km Race - May 11 to September 20, 2026

## Race Details
- **Event:** XCM 35km on MTB
- **Race Date:** Sunday, September 20, 2026
- **Expected duration:** ~1 h 30 min
- **Secondary race / tune-up:** Sunday, September 6, 2026 (tentative), with demands closer to the May 10 event
- **Primary goals:**
  1. Raise FTP from `275 W` toward `300 W` over the winter without over-relying on HIIT.
  2. Improve aerobic durability and resistance to late-race fade.
  3. Maintain punchy MTB repeatability for shorter climbs, exits, and re-accelerations.
  4. Build gym strength while protecting the ankle.

---

## Planning Principles
- **Bias:** more aerobic volume and gym strength, less frequent trainer HIIT.
- **Weekly pattern:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday riding available; Friday and Sunday mostly rest.
- **Intensity distribution target:** about `75-85%` easy aerobic, `10-15%` sub-threshold/threshold, `5-10%` high intensity.
- **Bike use:**
  - **Road bike:** clean aerobic volume and threshold work.
  - **Smart trainer:** precise FTP work when needed, but not as the center of the block.
  - **MTB:** technical work, race-specific pacing, and long rides.
- **Ankle rules:** prefer seated accelerations, avoid torque-heavy grinding, and reduce load if pain rises above `3/10` or next-morning stiffness increases.

---

## Macro Structure

### Phase 0 - Post-May Reset
- **Dates:** May 11-24
- **Goal:** recover from May racing, restore freshness, and start the winter block without carrying fatigue.
- **Weekly volume:** `4-6 h`
- **Main work:** easy Zone 2, mobility, and light return to gym.
- **Intensity:** none in the first week; one short controlled threshold touch in the second week only if fully recovered.

### Phase 1 - Base + Strength I
- **Dates:** May 25-June 21
- **Goal:** rebuild aerobic floor and start the heaviest gym period.
- **Weekly volume:** `7-9 h`
- **Bike priorities:**
  - `2` aerobic rides of `75-120 min`
  - `1` long ride of `2-3 h`
  - `1` controlled aerobic quality session each week
- **Quality session examples:**
  - `3 x 12 min @ 80-85% FTP`
  - `2 x 20 min @ 82-88% FTP`
  - `3 x 10 min upper Zone 2 / low tempo on climbs, all seated`
- **Gym focus:** `2` sessions/week, heavy but controlled
  - squat or leg press variation
  - Romanian deadlift or trap-bar deadlift
  - split squat or step-up
  - soleus/calf work
  - hip/core stability

### Phase 2 - Base + Strength II
- **Dates:** June 22-July 19
- **Goal:** extend durability and keep pushing aerobic development.
- **Weekly volume:** `8-10 h`
- **Bike priorities:**
  - increase long ride toward `3-3.5 h`
  - add more sustained aerobic power close to FTP but below it
  - MTB handling every `1-2` weeks
- **Quality session examples:**
  - `3 x 15 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - `2 x 20 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - `4 x 10 min @ 90% FTP with high cadence`
- **Gym focus:** `2` sessions/week, still strength-oriented but slightly less total fatigue than Phase 1.

### Phase 3 - FTP Build With Base Bias
- **Dates:** July 20-August 16
- **Goal:** turn the winter aerobic work into a higher sustainable power ceiling.
- **Weekly volume:** `8-10 h`
- **Bike priorities:**
  - `1` FTP-builder session each week
  - `1` secondary aerobic quality session
  - `1` long ride
  - `1` MTB skills/endurance ride when terrain and time allow
- **Quality session examples:**
  - `2 x 20 min @ 90-94% FTP`
  - `3 x 12 min @ 95-98% FTP`
  - `3 x 15 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - every `10-14` days: `6 x 1 min hard / 2.5 min easy` to keep punchiness awake
- **Gym focus:** `1-2` sessions/week, maintenance-heavy rather than overload-heavy.

### Phase 4 - Race-Specific Build
- **Dates:** August 17-September 6
- **Goal:** sharpen MTB repeatability and use the September 6 race as a specific tune-up.
- **Weekly volume:** `7-9 h`
- **Bike priorities:**
  - `1` structured punchy session
  - `1` threshold/FTP support session
  - `1` race-specific MTB session
  - `1` long endurance ride with simulation block
- **Quality session examples:**
  - `8-10 x 1 min @ 118-122% FTP / 2 min easy`
  - `2 sets of 8 x (40 s / 20 s)` only occasionally and only if ankle is calm
  - `3 x 10 min @ 95-100% FTP`
  - MTB laps with repeated short surges after corners or climbs

### Phase 5 - Recover, Rebuild, Taper To A Race
- **Dates:** September 7-20
- **Goal:** absorb the September 6 effort, then arrive fresh and sharp for the shorter September 20 event.
- **Weekly volume:** `5-7 h`
- **Bike priorities:**
  - first `2-3` days after Sep 6: recovery only
  - one threshold support session in race week minus `10-12` days
  - one short punchy activation in race week minus `4-5` days
  - one opener ride on race week Saturday

## Weekly Templates

### Template A - Base + Strength Week
- **Monday:** Gym session A + optional `30-45 min` easy spin
- **Tuesday:** Road/trainer aerobic quality (`3 x 12-15 min @ 80-90% FTP`)
- **Wednesday:** Road Zone 2 `90-120 min`, cadence `90-100 rpm`
- **Thursday:** Gym session B + `45-75 min` easy endurance or MTB skills
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long endurance ride `2-3.5 h`, mostly Zone 2
- **Sunday:** Rest

### Template B - FTP Build Week
- **Monday:** Gym maintenance + optional easy recovery spin
- **Tuesday:** FTP builder (`2 x 20 min` or `3 x 12 min`)
- **Wednesday:** Zone 2 `90-120 min`
- **Thursday:** Secondary quality (`tempo/threshold support` or short MTB skills)
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long ride `2.5-3.5 h` with late steady block
- **Sunday:** Rest

### Template C - Race-Specific Week
- **Monday:** Rest or `30-45 min` recovery spin
- **Tuesday:** Punchy session (`1 min reps` or short repeatability work)
- **Wednesday:** Zone 2 `75-105 min`
- **Thursday:** MTB race-specific session or threshold support
- **Friday:** Rest
- **Saturday:** Long MTB ride with one simulation block or recon laps
- **Sunday:** Rest

## Gym Guidelines
- **Session A - Main strength**
  - squat or trap-bar deadlift
  - split squat / step-up
  - calf + soleus loading
  - anti-rotation core
- **Session B - Posterior chain + stability**
  - Romanian deadlift
  - single-leg work
  - glute med / hip stability
  - foot/ankle control work
- **Loading guidance:**
  - early winter: `3-5` work sets of `4-6` reps on main lifts
  - later winter: reduce volume, keep strength touch
  - no failure reps
  - avoid adding impact-heavy plyometrics unless ankle tolerance is clearly stable

---

## Progression Targets
- **Aerobic durability:** reach regular comfort with `3-3.5 h` mostly Zone 2 rides.
- **FTP support:** spend more time at `88-95% FTP` before adding extra HIIT density.
- **Strength:** become stronger in main lower-body lifts without provoking ankle flare-ups.
- **Race specificity:** from August onward, shift one weekly session toward MTB punchiness and technical exits.

Realistic target by late September:
- clear FTP progress toward the high `280s` or `290s`
- stronger durability and better repeatability
- `300 W` remains a stretch target, but this approach should move you toward it without sacrificing consistency

---

## Monitoring Rules
- Keep pain during and after sessions at or below `3/10`.
- If morning stiffness rises after gym or punchy MTB work, reduce Thursday/Saturday intensity before cutting easy aerobic volume.
- If fatigue is high, remove the secondary quality session first.
- Every `4th` week, reduce volume by about `25-35%` and cut gym load slightly.
- Recheck FTP or field benchmarks every `6-8` weeks, not every week.

- **Best race-prep shift:** add more punchy MTB work from mid-August onward
