# Revised Season Plan - May to November 2026

## Main Targets
- **Primary race:** mid-November 2026 XCM on MTB, about `75 km` and about `3 h 30 min`.
- **Secondary races:** one or more shorter MTB races in September/October, about `1 h 30 min` to `2 h`.
- **Optional race:** another XCM at the end of November.
- **Main performance goal:** raise FTP from `285 W` toward `300-315 W` by November while staying healthy and consistent.

---

## Core Strategy
- Use a **practical endurance model**: mostly Zone 2, one key bike workout most weeks, and a second harder bike touch only when the phase becomes more race-specific.
- This is **polarized-leaning**, not strict polarized. In practice, you will still use some threshold and over-under work because it fits your FTP and XCM goals.
- Put the main focus on **aerobic volume, repeatable sub-threshold work, and long-ride durability**.
- Use **over-unders** as the main lactate-shuttle tool and **40/20s** as an occasional anaerobic maintenance tool, not the default every week.
- Keep the ankle safe: prefer seated power, avoid torque-heavy grinding, and back off if pain goes above `3/10` or next-morning stiffness rises.

---

## Weekly Rhythm
- **Monday:** Zone 2 ride plus gym, with the gym biased to lower body.
- **Tuesday:** Zone 2 ride.
- **Wednesday:** rest or regenerative spin.
- **Thursday:** key structured session, usually sub-threshold; add short race-specific intensity only when it helps the block.
- **Friday:** rest or regenerative spin plus gym, with the gym biased to upper body.
- **Saturday:** long ride, mostly Zone 2, with fueling practice.
- **Sunday:** full rest.

This schedule protects Thursday quality and Saturday durability better than forcing a traditional midweek intensity slot.

Zone 2 here means steady aerobic work that stays clearly below tempo: smooth breathing, no sustained surges, and no drifting into "hard but I can still justify it" effort.

---

## Volume Guidance
- Volume targets below are **bike hours**.
- If your real weekly cap including gym is around `8 h`, stay near the **low end** of each range.
- If life and recovery allow more, push selected build weeks toward the **high end**.
- Add volume before adding more intensity.

## Season Structure

### Phase 1 - Bridge Back In
- **Dates:** now to May 17
- **Goal:** absorb the cancelled-race taper and resume normal training without losing momentum.
- **Bike volume:** `6-7 h`
- **Intensity:** one short controlled Tuesday support session only if legs feel normal
- **Note:** if you already feel good, do not extend this phase

### Phase 2 - Aerobic Base + Strength
- **Dates:** May 18 to June 28
- **Goal:** rebuild the aerobic floor and make gym work productive without hurting bike quality.
- **Bike volume:** `7-9 h`
- **Bias:** mostly easy, with one structured Thursday session
- **Tuesday:** Zone 2 only
- **Key Thursday options:**
  - `3 x 12-15 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - `2 x 18 min` over-unders as `2 min @ 90-92%` / `1 min @ 98-102%`
  - every `10-14` days only: `2 x 8 x (40 s hard / 20 s easy)` at `118-122% FTP`, all seated
- **Saturday:** long ride `2.5-3.5 h`, mostly Zone 2

### Phase 3 - Base II + FTP Build
- **Dates:** June 29 to August 9
- **Goal:** turn the base work into a higher FTP without drifting into too much middle intensity.
- **Bike volume:** `8-10 h`
- **Bias:** one key Thursday session, one small weekend durability touch every second week
- **Tuesday:** Zone 2 only
- **Key Thursday options:**
  - `2 x 20 min @ 90-94% FTP`
  - `3 x 12-15 min` over-unders as `2 min @ 92-95%` / `1 min @ 100-102%`
  - every `10-14` days: `2 x 10 x (40 s / 20 s)` at `120-125% FTP`
- **Thursday:** mostly endurance; every second week add `4-6 x 1 min` hard / `2.5 min` easy, seated
- **Saturday:** long ride `3-4 h`, occasionally finishing with `20-40 min` strong aerobic pressure

### Phase 4 - Short-Race Support Block
- **Dates:** August 10 to October 4
- **Goal:** use September/October races as supported `B` races while preserving aerobic development for November.
- **Bike volume:** `7.5-9.5 h` in normal weeks, `5.5-7 h` in race weeks
- **Bias:** one structured Thursday session, one long ride in non-race weeks
- **Tuesday:** Zone 2 only
- **Key Thursday options:**
  - `40/20s`: `2 x 8-10 x (40 s / 20 s)` at `120-125% FTP`
  - `8-10 x 1 min @ 118-122% FTP / 2 min easy`
  - `3 x 10-12 min` over-unders as `2 min @ 95%` / `1 min @ 102-105%`
- **Thursday:** MTB session with short climbs, exits, traction, and technical pacing
- **Saturday:** race, race simulation, or long MTB endurance with a `20-40 min` race-feel block
- **Important:** keep one longer aerobic ride most weeks even during this phase

### Phase 5 - XCM Build To Mid-November
- **Dates:** October 5 to November 8
- **Goal:** convert shorter-race fitness into `75 km` XCM durability.
- **Bike volume:** `8.5-10.5 h`
- **Bias:** durability first, threshold support second, anaerobic maintenance third
- **Tuesday:** Zone 2 only
- **Key Thursday options:**
  - `2 x 25 min @ 88-92% FTP`
  - `3 x 15 min` over-unders as `3 min @ 92-95%` / `2 min @ 100-102%`
  - every `10-14` days: `6 x 1 min @ 118-122% FTP / 2.5 min easy`
- **Thursday:** short maintenance touch or MTB skills, not a deep workout
- **Saturday:** long MTB ride `3.5-4.5 h` with a `45-90 min` race-feel block after `90-120 min` easy
- **Specificity rule:** from early October onward, start rehearsing race-feel work under fatigue, not only when fresh

### Phase 6 - Taper To The Main Race
- **Dates:** final `10-12` days before the mid-November race
- **Bike volume:** `4.5-5.5 h`
- **Goal:** drop fatigue without losing sharpness
- **Keep:** one short Thursday sharpen, one Saturday opener, and easy riding in between
- **Drop:** heavy gym, extra threshold work, and any unnecessary long ride load

### Phase 7 - Optional Late-November XCM
- **Dates:** after the main race through late November
- **If racing again:** take `4-6` easy days, then one support Thursday, one light activation later in the week, and taper again
- **If not racing again:** use this as transition and recovery

## Gym And Bike Coordination Rules
- Monday is the main lower-body gym day because Tuesday is only Zone 2.
- Keep Thursday as the only structured bike quality day in this block.
- Friday gym must stay low-fatigue enough that Saturday still feels like the priority ride.
- In race weeks, Friday becomes mobility, trunk, and ankle work only.

---

## Race Handling Rules For September/October
- Treat shorter races as **supported training races**, not the peak of the season.
- In race week, reduce total volume by about `20-30%`.
- Keep Thursday as the only real workout; Tuesday stays easy.
- Use Thursday as activation, not fatigue.
- Skip lower-body lifting within `48 h` of the race.
- After the race, take `1-2` easy days, then return to the plan.

---

## Progress Checks And Decision Rules
- Recheck FTP or a field benchmark every `6-8` weeks.
- Judge progress by:
  - more time sustainable at `88-95% FTP`
  - better durability late in Saturday rides
  - improved repeatability in Thursday quality sessions
  - stable ankle response the next morning
- If Thursday power is flat for `2` weeks and legs feel heavy, reduce Monday gym and remove Friday lower-body work before adding more work.
- If long-ride recovery is good for `2-3` weeks in a row, add `30-45 min` of Zone 2 before adding more HIIT.
- If FTP is flat after `6-8` weeks but recovery is good, increase aerobic volume first.
- If ankle stiffness is rising for more than `1-2` weeks, replace `40/20s` with sub-threshold work.

---

## Load Management Rules
- Every `4th` week, cut bike volume by about `25-30%` and reduce gym load.
- Add volume gradually, not all at once.
- Protect the key sessions in this order:
  - Thursday quality
  - Saturday long ride
  - Tuesday aerobic support
- If you need to cut something, cut Thursday intensity first.

---

## Summary
- **Main goal:** build a bigger aerobic engine now, sharpen with shorter races, then peak for a mid-November XCM.
- **Best FTP levers:** consistent Zone 2, longer over-unders, enough total volume, disciplined fueling, and gym work that does not blunt bike quality.
- **Best anaerobic lever:** `40/20s` every `10-14` days, not constant VO2 overload.
- **Season priority order:** mid-November XCM first, late-November XCM second, September/October races third.
